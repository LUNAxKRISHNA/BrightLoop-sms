# Subscription Management System

A single platform to manage plans, subscriptions and recurring billing for a subscription-based business.

## Problem Statement

Brightloop Software sells all its products on subscription: business software billed monthly or yearly, optional add-ons, and products available in different variants. Its customers range from small shops on monthly plans to large companies on yearly contracts.

Today, everything is handled manually:

- Customer subscriptions are tracked in a spreadsheet.
- Renewals depend on whoever happens to notice.
- Invoices are typed up by hand every month.
- Payments are matched to invoices by scrolling through bank statements.
- Revenue reports are built manually at the end of each quarter.

As a result, renewals get missed, invoices go out wrong, and nobody can say how much recurring revenue the company actually has.

## Goal

Build one unified system that covers the full subscription lifecycle:

- **User accounts and roles:** Admin, internal staff, and customers with their own portal
- **Products and plans:** product variants and recurring plans billed daily, weekly, monthly or yearly
- **Quotations and subscriptions:** quotation templates and subscriptions that move from Draft to Closed
- **Invoicing:** automatic invoice generation with tax calculation
- **Discounts:** discount rules applied automatically
- **Payments:** payment recording and tracking of outstanding and overdue invoices
- **Reporting:** a dashboard for active subscriptions, revenue and overdue invoices
